→ encodeHTML(text)

→ prepareSecureData(data, version)

Validación en servidor:
→ ServerSecurityHelper.ValidateSafeString()

Parametrized Query:
→ cmd.Parameters.AddWithValue("@param", value)

→ monitorCSPViolations()
